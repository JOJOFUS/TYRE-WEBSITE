📏 How to See Your Tyre Size

Every tyre has a code printed on the sidewall — for example 205/55 R16 91 V. Understanding these numbers helps you choose the perfect fit for your vehicle, ensuring safety and performance.

⚡ EXAMPLE: 205/55 R16 91 V
🔹 Width (205) Tyre width in millimetres from sidewall to sidewall. 🔹 Profile (55) Sidewall height as a percentage of width. Lower = sportier handling. 🔹 Rim (R16) Radial construction & diameter of wheel in inches (16"). 🔹 Load (91) Load index: maximum weight capacity per tyre. 🔹 Speed (V) Speed rating: max safe speed (V = up to 240 km/h).
💡 Quick tip: Not sure about your tyre size? Check the driver's door jamb or your owner's manual. Or simply call us — we'll help you identify the right size!
